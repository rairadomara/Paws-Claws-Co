Measurable Community Impact

Stray animal overpopulation and lost pets place heavy operational strain on local barangays, animal control units, and volunteer rescue groups. By lowering the technical barrier to automated image sorting, PawDetect AI empowers neighborhoods to respond faster and more compassionately.

Because the application runs entirely on free, open web standards, any community group with a smartphone or laptop can deploy it immediately—zero installation or budget required.
